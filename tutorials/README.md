# feynsage tutorials

Short notebooks that go step by step. Open them with the SageMath kernel, in this order.

| # | Notebook | What you learn |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`01_first_steps.ipynb`](01_first_steps.ipynb) | load the package, ask a function what it does, the tadpole and the bubble, poles and finite parts, numbers above threshold, a plot |
| 2 | [`02_one_loop_tensors.ipynb`](02_one_loop_tensors.ipynb) | numerators with `loop()`, a tensor reduction checked by hand, triangles and boxes (symbols, 30 digits, dilogarithms), soft and collinear poles |
| 3 | [`03_graph_polynomials.ipynb`](03_graph_polynomials.ipynb) | a diagram as a graph, spanning trees and 2-forests with pictures, $\mathcal U$ and $\mathcal F$ at one, two and three loops, three independent checks, a number from the parametric formula |
| 4 | [`04_families_ibp_masters.ipynb`](04_families_ibp_masters.ipynb) | integral families, sectors, the IBP identities, reduction to master integrals, counting masters with critical points, the two-loop kite as $6\zeta(3)$ |
| 5 | [`05_dirac_traces_form.ipynb`](05_dirac_traces_form.ipynb) | gamma-matrix traces with FORM, $\gamma_5$, the full $e^+e^-\to\mu^+\mu^-$ cross section, the pion trace |

Every notebook ends with exercises. Each exercise has a solution you can open.

The longer tour of the whole package, with the pion lifetime and the QED of Chluba's thesis, is
[`../examples/feynsage_walkthrough.ipynb`](../examples/feynsage_walkthrough.ipynb). The physics behind every step is in the lecture note "Feynman integrals at one loop".
